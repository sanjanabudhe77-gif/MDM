Paintings

A visual art form where artists apply pigments and colors to flat surfaces like canvas, paper, or walls using brushes, knives, or sponges.

Abstract Painting

Abstract: is a style of art that uses colors, shapes, and brush marks to show feelings instead of real-life objects

Cubism Painting

Cubism: break subjects down into geometric cubes, shapes, and multiple overlapping perspectives at the same time.

Expressionism Painting

Expressionism: Distorts colors and shapes to show raw human feelings over clean accuracy.

Surrealism Painting

Surrealism: Mixes dream worlds and bizarre logic into weird, lifelike scenes.

Pop Art Painting

Pop Art: Uses famous faces, consumer goods, and bright commercial print.

Impressionism Painting

Impressionism: Uses short brush strokes and bright light to show a fleeting moment.
